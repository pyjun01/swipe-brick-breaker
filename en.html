Swipe Brick Breaker

A simple but addictive brick breaker game you can play with just a swipe. Aim and launch balls to smash numbered bricks — each brick needs to be hit that many times to break. As rounds go up, bricks get tougher and the grid fills up fast. No downloads, no installs. Just open your browser and start playing. Works great on mobile too.

FAQ

How do I play?

Just swipe in the direction you want to launch the balls. They'll bounce around and hit the bricks. Each brick has a number — that's how many hits it takes to break. Multiple balls fire at once, so angle matters!

Is it really free?

Yep, 100% free. No paywalls, no ads to unlock stuff. Just open the site and play.

Can I play on my phone?

Absolutely! It actually feels even better on mobile since you're swiping with your finger. Works on both iPhone and Android — any browser will do.

Do I need to install anything?

Nope. It runs right in your browser. Bookmark it if you want quick access — it'll feel just like an app.
